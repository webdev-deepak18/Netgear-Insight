## Goal
Deepak will build a full self-initiated UX case study on **one** NETGEAR product: design system → components → screens → prototype. This is Pass 1. Look at the top product for each audience at **medium depth** and score how well each would serve as a UX case study. Pass 2 will go deep on the one he picks.

**Focus on the software/app experience** (setup, management, monitoring), not marketing pages. The role is about turning complex networking into clear UX.

## Candidates (adjust if the site shows a better flagship per audience)
| Audience | Product + software surface |
|---|---|
| Home / families | Orbi mesh WiFi + **Orbi app** (and NETGEAR Armor / Smart Parental Controls) |
| Gamers / power users | Nighthawk router + **Nighthawk app** (and the router web UI) |
| Small/medium business | Insight-managed switches/APs + **NETGEAR Insight** (cloud web + mobile) |
| Mobile / on-the-go | Nighthawk M-series mobile hotspot + its app |
| Pro AV (if notable) | AV-line switches + **Engage controller** |

## For each candidate
1. **Product & software surface:** what the app or portal does, its platforms, and its key flows (onboarding/setup, dashboard, device list, settings, alerts, subscriptions).
2. **Visible UX:** screenshots or descriptions from the app store listings, KB articles and YouTube walkthroughs. Note the IA of the main screens.
3. **User pain points:** the top 5 recurring complaints from the Play Store, App Store, community.netgear.com and Reddit (r/HomeNetworking, r/Netgear, r/networking). Include short quotes and links.
4. **Competitor benchmark:** 1–2 direct competitors (eero, TP-Link Deco/Omada, UniFi, Meraki) and what they do better on UX.
5. **Strategic weight:** how much NETGEAR is investing in this line (earnings calls, launches, subscription push).
6. **Public material available:** enough screenshots, docs and videos to redesign it credibly without access to the product?

## Scoring (1–5 each, then total)
- Fit to the JD: complex networking concepts, telemetry, enterprise/consumer, multi-platform.
- Fit to Deepak's strengths: B2B SaaS dashboards, design systems, code-savvy. Also fills his gaps: mobile, networking domain.
- Strength of the problem: clear, evidenced pain points.
- Strategic relevance to NETGEAR right now.
- How much public material exists to work from.

## Output format
- One section per candidate, about 300–400 words each, in bullets, with source URLs.
- A **scoring table** with all candidates.
- **Recommendation:** the top pick and runner-up, with 3 reasons each, and the specific problem the case study should tackle (e.g. "first-time Insight onboarding for a 10-person office").
- End with **"Implications for Deepak"** (3–5 bullets).
