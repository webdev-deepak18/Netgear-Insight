# 001: NETGEAR Deep Dive (company, products, UX surface)
status: open
output: research/gemini/001-netgear-deep-dive.md

## Goal
Deepak has a round-2 interview with NETGEAR's Sr. UX Director around Tue 13 Oct 2026, for a UX Designer role in Bengaluru. He needs to understand NETGEAR deeply: the business, the products, and especially the **software and UX surfaces** a UX designer would work on. Note: the US site (netgear.com) may redirect from India. Use the alternative sources listed below.

## Questions to answer
1. **Company now:** business segments (for example Connected Home vs NETGEAR for Business/NFB, and the service/subscription side). Recent revenue trend, CEO and leadership strategy, priorities stated in the last 2–3 earnings calls (e.g. pivot to enterprise, subscriptions, WiFi 7). Major news from the last 12 months, including acquisitions, launches and layoffs.
2. **Product lines:** a short map of Orbi, Nighthawk, Insight-managed business switches/APs, Pro AV, mobile hotspots, Meural, etc. Mark which ones are on sale in India (netgear.in).
3. **Software and apps (most important):** the Nighthawk app, Orbi app, Insight (cloud + mobile), NETGEAR Armor/security, Smart Parental Controls, and any newer ones. For each one:
   - What it does.
   - Platforms.
   - App store ratings.
   - The **top 5 recurring user complaints and praises** from Play Store and App Store reviews and community.netgear.com.
4. **Design and UX signals:** any public trace of NETGEAR's design team or design system, plus UX/design job posts, Dribbble/Behance work by NETGEAR designers, conference talks, and blog posts. Also any AI features in their products or apps.
5. **India / Bengaluru:** what NETGEAR does in Bengaluru (an R&D/engineering center?), its size, and which products it works on, if public.
6. **Competitors:** how the UX of TP-Link Deco/Omada, eero, Ubiquiti UniFi, Cisco Meraki and ASUS compares with NETGEAR's apps. Focus on setup and management UX.

## Scope & sources
- Use the last 12–18 months. Mark anything older.
- Sources:
  - investor.netgear.com (10-K, earnings call transcripts)
  - netgear.in
  - netgear.com/business and insight.netgear.com
  - kb.netgear.com
  - community.netgear.com
  - Google Play and the Apple App Store
  - the newsroom and press releases
  - reputable tech press (The Verge, PCMag, Tom's Guide, etc.)
  - web.archive.org if netgear.com redirects
- Skip: deep hardware specs, pricing tables, and individual employees' personal details.

## Output format
- Organize the sections by question number. Use bullets that are easy to scan, about 1500–2000 words in total.
- Every fact gets a source URL.
- Add a short table for Q3: App | Platforms | Rating | Top complaints | Top praises.
- End with **"Implications for Deepak"**, 5–7 bullets on:
  - the UX problems he could raise in the interview,
  - which of his strengths match where NETGEAR is heading,
  - smart questions to ask the hiring manager.
