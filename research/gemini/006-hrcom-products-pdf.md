# HR.com Products (PDF Digest)

**20-second spoken answer (What does HR.com do?):** 
"According to its product guide, HR.com is a dual-sided platform. It provides HR professionals with free memberships, webcasts, and certification prep to advance their careers. At the same time, it helps HR solution providers generate qualified sales leads through custom research, email marketing, webcasts, and virtual event sponsorships."

## 1. Summary
HR.com serves two main audiences: **HR professionals (HRPs)** and **vendors (HR solution providers)**. For HRPs, it acts as an educational hub offering webcasts, networking, and certification credits to help them manage their offices and stay compliant (p. 3, 8, 9). For vendors, HR.com serves as a marketing channel to reach decision-makers, offering tools for brand awareness, custom research, and the generation of sales-qualified leads (p. 1, 2, 4).

## 3. Sponsor/Vendor Packages
- **Lead Generation Flow:** Vendors can hire HR.com to conduct **Custom Research** or sponsor existing **State of the Industry Research** (p. 1). They can then present this research at a 1-day **Virtual Event** to generate "tangible sales leads" and "sales qualified leads" from a targeted audience (p. 1, 4).
- Vendors also use **Webcasts** for 1-hour live presentations to engage audiences and generate leads (p. 3).
- **Buyer Activation Program:** Vendors get access to a fully managed email marketing program targeting a list of decision-makers to filter qualified leads (p. 2).
- *Note: ePublications are not in the PDF.*

## 4. Member Offerings
- **Education & Certification:** Access to live and archived webcasts to earn HRCI/SHRM recertification credits (p. 3, 9). Certification prep boasts a 93% pass rate (p. 9).
- **Membership Tiers:** 
  - **Free Membership:** Delivers free and unlimited webcasts (p. 8).
  - **Prime Membership:** A premium tier providing thousands of recorded training videos on sensitive HR topics (e.g., terminations, sexual harassment) and acting as a tool for performance reviews and salary insights (p. 8).

## 5. Software / Platform / SaaS Tools
- **HR Virtual Events (PLATFORM):** An online platform acting as a virtual tradeshow where users can visit booths, watch demos, chat, and "shake hands virtually" (p. 7). 
- *No other SaaS tools (like ATS or Nexthire) are in the PDF.*

## 6. Scale Numbers
- **85 clients** over the past 5 years with a **90% renewal rate** for the Buyer Activation Program (p. 2).
- **Over 5000+ virtual events** hosted in the last 20 years (p. 4).
- **Over 180,000 HR Executives** turn to HR.com for education (p. 4).
- **14 recertification credits** minimum earned at HR West (p. 5).
- **93% pass rate** for Certification Preparation (p. 9).
- *(Total member count is not in the PDF).*

## 7. Unexpected or New Findings
- **"Buyer Activation Program"** is the new name for the "Managed Email Campaign" (p. 2).
- **"Buyer Intent"** is listed as an overlay rather than a standalone product (p. 2).
- The absence of "ePublications" and "Nexthire" in a 2022 brand guide suggests these might not be primary flagship products marketed directly under this specific umbrella.
