# HR.com Overview

**20-second spoken answer (What does HR.com do?):** 
"HR.com is one of the largest professional networks and educational platforms for Human Resources practitioners. It provides HR professionals with free resources like research reports, certification prep, and virtual events to build their careers, while making money by offering lead-generation and sponsorship packages to B2B HR tech vendors who want to reach that audience."

## 1. What is HR.com?
- **Main business:** A digital-first professional network, educational platform, and media company for the human resources industry. 
- **Customers:** A two-sided market. Users are HR professionals (practitioners, leaders, executives) who consume content. Buyers are B2B HR tech vendors and solution providers who pay to advertise to that audience.
- [Source: HR.com Advertise](https://www.hr.com/en/advertise/)

## 2. Business Lines
- **Membership/Community:** A free-to-join network for HR professionals to connect and access resources.
- **HR Certification and Education:** Webcasts and courses offering SHRM/HRCI recertification credits, plus exam prep.
- **HR Research Institute:** Publishes industry research reports and infographics (funded by sponsors).
- **Virtual Events & Webcasts:** Themed sets of webcasts where research findings and thought leadership are presented.
- **ePublications (ePubs):** Monthly digital magazines (now transitioning to weekly newsletters) covering specific niches like Talent Acquisition and Leadership.
- **Sponsorship/Lead Generation:** The B2B advertising arm helping vendors reach HR buyers.
- [Source: HR.com Advertise](https://www.hr.com/en/advertise/) | [Source: HR.com About](https://www.hr.com/en/about_us/)

## 3. Revenue Model (How it makes money)
- **Primary Revenue:** Sponsorship and lead-generation packages sold to HR tech vendors.
- **Mechanism:** Vendors pay to sponsor research initiatives, host webcasts, or place ads in ePubs/newsletters in exchange for qualified leads (e.g., attendees of a sponsored virtual event) and brand visibility among HR.com's members.
- [Source: HR.com Advertise](https://www.hr.com/en/advertise/)
