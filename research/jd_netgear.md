## 🎯 Role Overview
As a UX Designer at NETGEAR, you will craft intuitive, engaging interfaces for enterprise and consumer networking hardware and software products. Partnering with Product Management, Engineering, and Visual Design teams, you will translate complex networking concepts into clear, user-centered experiences across web, mobile, and desktop platforms.

---

## 📋 Key Responsibilities
* **User Research:** Plan and conduct usability studies, interviews, and surveys to uncover user needs and networking configuration pain points.
* **Interaction Design:** Develop wireframes, user flows, and high-fidelity interactive prototypes that illustrate product functionality and system telemetry.
* **Visual & Information Design:** Create high-fidelity mockups and design specifications aligned with NETGEAR's brand and component libraries.
* **AI-Assisted Prototyping:** Utilize tools such as Claude, ChatGPT, and Figma AI workflows to accelerate ideation, UX writing, and rapid functional prototyping.
* **Engineering Collaboration:** Work closely with engineers to ensure designs are implemented accurately and responsively across devices and operating systems.
* **Design Systems & Documentation:** Maintain and evolve UX guidelines, tokenized component libraries, and style documentation.

---

## 🛠️ Required Skills & Qualifications
* 10+ years of complete UX Design experience on B2B, SaaS, enterprise, or networking products.
* Strong proficiency with Figma (components, auto-layout, design tokens, variables, and prototypes).
* Hands-on experience with AI-assisted prototyping workflows (Claude, ChatGPT).
* Solid foundation in information architecture, typography, color, layout, and responsive web/mobile strategies.
* Basic understanding of HTML/CSS and responsive design principles.
* Strong communication skills with an analytical mindset focused on data-driven design decisions.
