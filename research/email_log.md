## 📄 Interview Prep PDF Summary
File: [NETGEAR_Candidate_Interview_Prep-.pdf](NETGEAR_Candidate_Interview_Prep-.pdf)

**NETGEAR values:**
1. **Dare to Transform:** Push boundaries. Embrace bold innovation.
2. **Connect and Delight:** Put customers first. Deliver products that improve lives.
3. **Communicate Courageously:** Speak openly. Build trust through honest dialogue.
4. **Collaborate to Win It Together:** Share accountability. Win as one team.

**Format:** A mix of behavioral/situational, skill-related and value-based questions. Usually 30–45 min per round. Structure: introductions, then focus-area questions, then your questions. It's meant as a two-way conversation, so come with questions.

**Answer framework:** STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result), with results quantified where possible.

**Before:**
* Research NETGEAR's products, recent news and the team.
* Prepare 2–3 stories that connect directly to the role.
* Bring talking points and questions.
* Do a tech check: camera, mic, connection.

**Day of:**
* Dress business casual.
* Use a tidy background with good lighting.
* Log on 5 min early.
* Pausing is fine.
* If running late, contact the recruiting coordinator.

**After:**
* Send a brief, specific, genuine thank-you note within 24 hrs.
* A polite follow-up is fine after 1–2 weeks of silence.
* Reflect on what went well.
