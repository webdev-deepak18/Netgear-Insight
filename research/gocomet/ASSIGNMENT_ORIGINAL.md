## About GoComet

GoComet is building an **AI-native platform for global trade**. Global enterprises use GoComet to manage freight across the entire journey, from planning and procurement to shipment tracking, disruption management and invoice validation.

Refer to the website to understand the product in detail: https://www.gocomet.com/

Two AI-native products are:

- **Nova** sets up and coordinates GoComet based on each company's processes, systems, partners and rules.
- **AI Workers** handle specific operational tasks, such as checking invoices, following up with carriers or comparing freight rates. People define the rules and approve important decisions.

As GoComet grows from a logistics software company into an AI-native enterprise company, we want to build a stronger and more consistent design language across our **brand, product, presentations and videos**.

Our logo and existing colours will stay the same. Your job is to think about the **design system around them**, and ensure consistent design flows across all the channels & creatives.

### Who do we sell to?

We sell to enterprise companies (refer website for exact logos), specifically logistics & supply chain departments. So, the key people who often evaluate GoComet are: Head, VP, or Director of Supply Chain & Logistics, and Digital Transformation teams.

## Assignment

**Guidelines:**
1. Use GoComet's public website for our logo and colours. The design should feel credible, sophisticated and technology-led, avoid generic AI clichés.
2. Use placeholder content wherever needed. Don't use any other company's brand assets.
3. Prioritise thinking and quality - that shows your design taste.

**Your work remains yours. We will not use the assignment commercially.**

## Task 1: Build GoComet Design System

GoComet's logo and colours stay the same. Design the system underneath them that makes GoComet look like an AI-native enterprise company.

**1. Foundations**
- How you would use our existing colours
- Additional colours for charts, data, alerts and dark backgrounds
- Typography and type sizes
- Grid and spacing
- Image style
- Icon style

**2. Core design components**

Create around **5 reusable components**.

For example:
- Proof / numbers section
- Quote card
- Section heading
- Chart style
- CTA
- "work in progress" device that shows an AI Worker carrying out a task

You can choose the components you think would be most useful. You can use these components later in the assignment.

**3.** Create a short **Do / Don't** page that someone outside the design team could understand and follow.

## Task 2: Create a Sales Deck Template

Using the design system from Task 1, create **4 slides** that could become part of a GoComet master sales deck.

**Create:**

**1. Title slide**
Heading: The AI-Native Platform for Global Trade
Subheading: Plan. Procure. Move. Resolve. Pay.

**2. Problem slide**
Show the problem of freight decisions being spread across:
- Emails
- Spreadsheets
- Carrier portals
- ERP workarounds

**3. Proof slide**
Show these four numbers:
- **10M** containers a year
- **500+** enterprise customers
- **70+** countries
- **5,000+** carrier integrations

**4. Flexible content slide**
Create one layout that Sales could reuse for different products, use cases or customer stories. Idea is to make this a reusable slide that Sales team can use.

**Final Question:** In this deck, which elements would you lock, and which would you leave editable for Sales?

## Task 3: Redesign a section of GoComet webpage

Pick **one page** from gocomet.com:
- Homepage, or
- Any product page

**First, give us a short critique**

Maximum **5 points** covering:
- What works well
- What could be improved
- What currently makes the page feel less enterprise-grade

What outcome would you see with the changes suggested above?

**Then redesign:**

Only the **first screen** of the page:
- Hero section
- Section immediately below it

Use the design system you created in Task 1.

## Task 4: Show How an AI Worker Works

Create **one visual** — this could be a web section, presentation slide or animation frame - your choice.

The visual should explain this workflow to a **Finance Controller in under 10 seconds**:

> An invoice arrives from a carrier.
> An AI Worker checks it against the contracted rate and shipment data.
> It finds an overcharge and flags it.
> The issue is sent to a person for approval before payment.

**Show clearly:**
- What the **AI Worker** does
- What the **person** decides
